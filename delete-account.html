Delete your Khurak Fit account

Deleting your account removes your sign-in and your online backup straight away. It can't be undone.

The quickest way: in the app

  1. Open Khurak Fit and tap Me.
  2. Tap Account & backup, then Delete my account.
  3. Type your password and tap Delete account and backup.

To also erase the data on your phone, tap Also erase everything on this phone, or use Me → Reset app.

Or delete it here

Sign in with the email and password you use in Khurak Fit.

What gets deleted

  • Your account (email address and sign-in).
  • Your online backup: profile, health details, food log, workouts, steps, weight log and photos.
  • AI usage counts are removed automatically within 2 days.

Data on your phone stays until you reset or uninstall the app. Can't sign in? Email support@khurakfit.com from the address on your account and we'll delete it for you within 30 days.
