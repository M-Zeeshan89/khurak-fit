Questions

Good to know.

Is Khurak Fit free?

Yes. Diet plans, the calorie counter, reminders, workouts and the step counter are free to use.

When is it on Google Play?

The Android app is in early testing right now. Want to try it before launch? Email support@khurakfit.com with the Gmail address you use on your phone and we'll add you.

Does it work on iPhone?

Yes. Open khurakfit.com/app in Safari, tap Share, then Add to Home Screen. It opens like an app and works offline. Background steps and reminders are Android only.

Which food does it use?

Food that's normal in your country, from South Asian and Middle Eastern to Western meals. It also adjusts to the season.

Is my data private?

Your plan and logs stay on your phone. Nothing is uploaded unless you choose to sign in for backup. Read the privacy policy.

Is this medical advice?

No. Khurak Fit gives general diet and fitness guidance. If you're pregnant, have a health condition or take medicine, talk to your doctor before changing your diet or exercise.

How do I delete my account?

In the app, go to Me → Account & backup → Delete my account, or use the delete account page.
