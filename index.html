Everything in one app

A diet that fits your kitchen, not someone else's.

Most diet apps hand you meals you'll never cook. Khurak Fit builds your plan from food that's normal where you live, then helps you stick to it every day.

Diet plan

Your meals, planned for every day.

Tell Khurak Fit your age, weight, goal and country. It plans breakfast, lunch, dinner and snacks with real portions and calories.

  • Food from your country, swapped to what's in season
  • Day, week and month views
  • Swap any meal you don't like
Smart reminders

It tells you what to eat, and when.

Reminders show the exact meal, what to skip and what's missing from your kitchen. If you've already done it, it stays quiet.

  • Meals, water, workouts and sleep
  • Tap a reminder to jump straight to it
  • No reminder for things you've already done
Home workout coach

A coach who shows you every move.

Follow a moving coach through 28-day home or gym programs. It starts easy and gets harder each week, with a timer and music.

  • No equipment needed for home plans
  • 20–35 minutes a day
  • Male and female coaches
Calorie counter

Count calories without the homework.

Type any food, snap a photo or scan the barcode on a packet. Khurak Fit shows the calories, protein, carbs and fat for the amount you ate.

  • Barcode scanner for packaged food
  • Daily calories left, at a glance
  • Protein goal that matches your plan
Step counter

Steps counted automatically. No watch needed.

On Android, Khurak Fit counts your steps in the background and shows them on a home-screen widget. You can also log steps yourself.

  • Home-screen widget with your daily ring
  • Distance, calories burned and active minutes
  • Last 7 days at a glance
Coach chat

Ask anything, in your own language.

"What should I eat next?" "How many calories do I have left?" The coach answers from your own plan, in English, Urdu, Arabic or Hindi.

  • Answers based on your plan and your day
  • Replies in the language you choose

Questions

Good to know.

Is Khurak Fit free?

Yes. Diet plans, the calorie counter, reminders, workouts and the step counter are free to use.

When is it on Google Play?

The Android app is in early testing right now. Want to try it before launch? Email support@khurakfit.com with the Gmail address you use on your phone and we'll add you.

Does it work on iPhone?

Yes. Open khurakfit.com/app in Safari, tap Share, then Add to Home Screen. It opens like an app and works offline. Background steps and reminders are Android only.

Which food does it use?

Food that's normal in your country, from South Asian and Middle Eastern to Western meals. It also adjusts to the season.

Is my data private?

Your plan and logs stay on your phone. Nothing is uploaded unless you choose to sign in for backup. Read the privacy policy.

Is this medical advice?

No. Khurak Fit gives general diet and fitness guidance. If you're pregnant, have a health condition or take medicine, talk to your doctor before changing your diet or exercise.

How do I delete my account?

In the app, go to Me → Account & backup → Delete my account, or use the delete account page.
