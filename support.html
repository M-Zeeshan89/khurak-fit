Quick fixes

Steps not counting

Allow "Physical activity" for Khurak Fit in your phone's settings, and turn off battery saving for the app.

Reminders not showing

Allow notifications and "Alarms & reminders" for Khurak Fit, then open the app once.

Changing phone

On the old phone go to Me → Account & backup and sign in. Sign in with the same account on the new phone.

Using an iPhone

Open khurakfit.com/app in Safari, tap Share, then Add to Home Screen. It opens like an app and works offline.

Wrong language

Go to Me → Language and choose English, اردو, العربية or हिन्दी.

Android early access

Email support@khurakfit.com with the Gmail address you use on your phone, and we'll add you to the test.

In the app you can also use Me → Send feedback, which adds your phone details for you.
