Quick fixes

  • Steps not counting: allow "Physical activity" for Khurak Fit in your phone's settings, and turn off battery saving for the app.
  • Reminders not showing: allow notifications and "Alarms & reminders" for Khurak Fit, then open the app once.
  • Changing phone: on the old phone go to Me → Account & backup and sign in. Sign in with the same account on the new phone.
  • iPhone: open khurakfit.com/app in Safari, tap Share, then Add to Home Screen.
  • Wrong language: Me → Language. You can choose English, اردو, العربية or हिन्दी.
