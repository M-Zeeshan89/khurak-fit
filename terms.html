1. Not medical advice

Khurak Fit gives general diet, nutrition and fitness guidance for healthy adults. It is not a doctor, dietitian or medical service, and it doesn't diagnose or treat any condition. Talk to a qualified health professional before you start a new diet or exercise program, especially if you are pregnant or breastfeeding, under 18, have a health condition, an eating disorder, or take medicine. Stop exercising and get help if you feel pain, dizziness or shortness of breath.

2. Results

Calories, portions, step counts and other numbers are estimates. Results differ from person to person, and we don't promise any particular weight loss or fitness result.

3. Your account

  • Keep your password private. You're responsible for activity on your account.

4. Using Khurak Fit fairly

5. AI features
