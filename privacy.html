Last updated: 10 November 2026

Khurak Fit helps you plan meals, count calories, train and track your progress. This policy explains what information the app uses, where it goes, and how you can delete it. We wrote it in plain words.

The short version

  • Your plan, food log, workouts and health details are stored on your phone.
  • If you create an account, a backup of that data is kept in Google Firebase so you can move to a new phone.
  • If you use the AI coach or AI photo scan, the message or photo is sent to our AI server and to Anthropic (the AI provider) to create a reply.
  • We don't sell your data, we don't show ads, and we don't use tracking or advertising IDs.
  • You can delete your account and backup at any time, in the app or on our website.

Information you give us

  • Profile: name or nickname, age, sex, height, weight, target weight, goal, activity level, body shape, country, food style, diet type.
  • Health details you choose to add: health conditions (for example diabetes or high blood pressure), pregnancy or menopause stage, fasting choice. These are used only to adjust your calories, food warnings and exercises.
  • Tracking: meals eaten, extra foods, water, sleep hours, weight log, workouts done, points and streaks, grocery stock.
  • Photos: a profile photo (kept on your phone and in your backup) and meal or fridge photos you take. Meal photos used for tap-to-count, and your before & after photos, stay on your phone and are never uploaded or backed up. Photos you send to the AI scan go to the AI server only to be analysed and are not kept there.
  • Account: your email address and a password. Passwords are handled by Google Firebase Authentication; we never see them.

Information the app collects on the phone

  • Steps: read from your phone's step sensor after you allow "Physical activity". On Android, Google Play services also records your step count on your phone (no Google account needed) so steps are counted while the app is closed. Steps stay on your phone and in your backup.
  • Country: guessed from your phone's language and time zone settings to suggest local foods. We don't use GPS location.
  • Food pictures: to show what a food looks like, the app loads public pictures from Wikipedia. Wikipedia sees a normal web request (your IP address), not your data.

Where your data goes

  • Google Firebase (Authentication and Realtime Database) stores your account and backup. Each backup can only be read by the signed-in owner with a confirmed email.
  • Our AI server (hosted on Cloudflare) checks that you're signed in, applies daily limits, and passes your AI question or photo, together with a short summary of your plan (age, goal, targets, health notes), to Anthropic to create the reply. The server keeps only a daily usage count, not your messages.
  • Anthropic processes these requests to provide the service under its commercial terms and does not use them to train its models.

Data is sent over encrypted connections (HTTPS).

How long we keep it

  • Data on your phone stays until you delete it (Me → Reset app) or uninstall the app.
  • Your online backup stays until you delete your account. When you delete it, the account and backup are removed straight away.
  • AI usage counts are deleted after 2 days.

Your choices and rights

  • Use it without an account: everything except backup and the AI coach works without signing in.
  • Download your data: Me → Account → Save file gives you a copy of everything.
  • Correct it: change any detail in Me.
  • Delete it: in the app, Me → Account → Delete my account. Or use the account deletion page on our website. To erase the copy on your phone, use Me → Reset app.

Children

Khurak Fit is for people aged 13 and over. Teens get full-calorie plans without weight-loss cutting. We don't knowingly collect data from children under 13. If you think a child under 13 has created an account, contact us and we'll delete it.

Health information

Khurak Fit gives general guidance, not medical advice. It doesn't diagnose or treat any condition. Talk to your doctor before changing your diet or exercise, especially if you are pregnant or have a health condition.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update the date at the top and show a notice in the app for important changes.

Contact

Questions or requests: support@khurakfit.com
